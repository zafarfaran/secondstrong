# Today tab (SS-0025)

Status: built in the prototype (`SSToday`). Chosen from four approaches in `design/app/dashboard.html`: the full-bleed one.
Spec: section 5 (five tabs), section 4.10 (the five habits), section 3 (Free sees calories, protein and steps only).

## What it shows

- **Hero.** A paper scene edge to edge, morning scene until late afternoon and dusk scene after, light and dark (generated by
  `design/onboarding/paper/gen_dashboard.py`). The coach stands on the scene's flat hill and plays its clips; the greeting is top left.
- **The coach's few words** under the greeting (`TodayLines`, two short lines, UK English, no exclamation marks) and an "Ask Miso" pill.
  Tapping the coach, the words or the pill opens the Coach tab.
- **A paper sheet** over the scene: protein first (big number, hairline bar), calories left and steps, the five habits as line icons
  (a ticked habit fills dark), and today's session with Start. In calorie-free mode the calories slot shows habits done instead.
- **Floating tab bar:** Food, Train, Today (a house icon, in the middle), Coach, Progress. Food, Train and Progress are placeholders.

## Coach tab

The coach waves from the scene, then the conversation: its opening line (greeting, her protein gap), three quick replies and a
field to write in. Replies are canned until the real coach is connected (SS-0025); `TodayLines` has the draft copy.

## Rules kept

Weight never appears. No streaks, scores or badges for weight or a deficit; habits are plain ticks. Nothing is stored or sent.
The sample member is made up (`TodaySummary.sample`): fake data only.

## Wiring

`SSToday` imports only `SSCore` and `SSDesign`. The app and the preview window (the composition roots) show `OnboardingView`
and switch to `MainTabsView` when the last onboarding button, Go to Today, is tapped. `make today` opens the preview on Today.

## Open

Time-of-day lines and the dusk scene follow the phone's clock; the Free plan view (dimmed Pro parts) and the real data sources
(Apple Health steps, food log, programme) are not built. Coach and clinical review of any coach line is still needed.

## Profile and settings (SS-0027)

A dark initial disc top right on every tab opens the profile (`ProfileView`), designed in `design/app/profile.html`: identity, her coach,
then You (plan and numbers, training, health answers), Reminders, Family (helpers, subscription), App (units, appearance, privacy and
data, support). Your plan and numbers shows protein and the daily target, and a **Show calories** switch that turns calories off or back
on everywhere (spec section 7). Privacy and data has the consent switches, download, doctor summary, deletion and support resources.
Built: the layout, the calories switch and the switches. Not connected yet, and said so in the prototype: editing the numbers, changing
coach, sign out, download, delete, units and appearance (shown, not changeable).

## Photos in the chat (SS-0028)

The camera button in the Coach tab's message bar opens the photo picker. A sent photo shows in the thread, the coach says "Looking…",
then replies with what it sees (`MealEstimate`: a name, protein and calories, always called an estimate) and an Add to today / Not quite
card. Add to today puts the protein (and calories) into Today once; in calorie-free mode the card shows protein only. The scanner is
**not connected**: results are made-up meals that cycle (`MealEstimate.samples`). The photo stays on the phone in the prototype.
Open: the real vision model and its cost, where photos go and how long they are kept (health-adjacent PII, see
[data handling](../business/data-handling.md)), camera permission wording, barcode and voice logging, and Free's three scans a week (spec section 3).

## Coach tab is full screen (SS-0026)

The tab bar is hidden on the Coach tab: the chat is its own screen with a back arrow to Today and one message pill. In the pill, a plain
+ opens a small menu (Photo, Scan a meal) and the waveform starts voice (it becomes send once she types). Chosen from `design/app/coach-bar.html` (C).

## How the coach answers: stages and streaming (SS-0029)

An answer arrives in stages, then streams. A text reply: Thinking, then Writing. A photo: Thinking, Looking at your photo, Spotting the
food, Estimating portions, then Writing. While it works, the coach's slot shows three pulsing dots and the current stage in a short plain
line, with the finished stages ticked above it (still dots under Reduce Motion).

**Smooth streaming.** The answer is written as one steady flow of letters by the clock (about 38 a second, easing in over the first
moment), not word by word. The whole text is laid out from the start, so the bubble is its final size and nothing reflows, and the newest
nine letters fade in rather than pop. Reduce Motion shows it at once. The meal card appears when it finishes, and the thread keeps the
end in sight as it grows. `ResponsePlan` and `TokenStream` (SSCore, tested) hold the stages and the pacing; `WritingBubble` draws it.

**Stop.** While the coach works the waveform or send button becomes a stop square. Stopping keeps what had arrived, cut back to the last
whole word, marked "Stopped" with Ask again.

**Failed.** A text answer that breaks part way keeps its words and says "Couldn't reach Miso. Nothing was lost." with Try again; a photo it
cannot read says "I couldn't read that photo. A clearer one might help." with Try again. The copy is calm and blames no one
(`ResponseFailure`, tested). In the prototype, type `/fail` to break the next text answer and `/failphoto` before sending a photo to break
that scan. The real service's events, retry limits and an offline state are still to design.

## Swipe between tabs (SS-0031)

Swipe left or right on the main screens to move one tab along the bar (food, train, today, coach, progress). The page follows the finger and the
next one slides in beside it; letting go past 60 points or with a quick flick completes the move, otherwise it springs back. The ends resist and
do not wrap, and it waits while the profile, check-ins or voice screens are open. Only a mostly sideways drag counts, so scrolling is untouched.

The full-screen chat has no tab bar, so its back arrow became a named pill, "Today" with a house, top left (option A in `design/app/coach-nav.html`).
Not tried on a device.

The tab bar shows where a swipe is heading (option C in `design/app/page-switch.html`): a soft capsule under the current icon glides along with the
finger, and the icons it passes darken. On the Coach tab the bar is hidden, so it only appears while a swipe to or from the chat is under way.
