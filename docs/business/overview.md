# Business overview

One page. Detail lives in the spec; use the [spec map](spec-map.md) to read a slice.

## Product

- **Positioning:** "Your strength and nutrition coach."
- **Sub-line:** Eat enough protein. Build muscle. Feel stronger. Stay healthy for
  the years ahead.
- **Target user:** women at any stage of life, including through perimenopause
  and after it.
- **Brand rule:** lead with the product name everywhere users see it. "Health"
  stays in the legal entity, domain and footer, so the brand does not read as
  medical.
- **Name:** Second Strong (decided 2026-10-20). The spec, `assets/` and the
  legal entity name still say Keel / Keel Health Ltd. Treat Keel as the old name
  and use Second Strong in new code and copy. Trademark clearance for the new name
  is not recorded here; see [open questions](../open-questions.md).

## Thesis

The coaching is built on a protein and strength framework. The quiz teaches it,
the plan reveal leads with the user's protein gap, and the app coaches against it.

## Pricing (summary)

- Free tier plus Pro: weekly, monthly, annual, lifetime. All Pro plans carry the
  same features. Annual is pre-selected on the paywall.
- 7-day free trial on every subscription plan, none on lifetime. All plans sit
  in one subscription group.
- No countdown timers or fake scarcity. Renewal price and cancellation must be
  clear and findable. Details: spec section 1.

## Acquisition funnel

Paid ad -> quiz (about 26-30 questions, 6 blocks) -> "building your plan"
moment -> email capture -> plan reveal -> paywall -> app with plan pre-filled.
Commitment is built before price is shown. Details: spec section 2.

## Non-negotiables

- Safety screening in the quiz is mandatory and is not a conversion lever.
- Projections are conservative ranges, labelled as estimates, and never shown
  to users in calorie-free mode or who screened positive.
- Never fabricate user numbers or testimonials.
- Health data is PII: [data handling](data-handling.md).
