Second Strong · cold start

From the icon to Today.

What happens between tapping the icon and seeing your day. iOS zooms the icon open into the launch screen, a still frame built from the icon itself. When the app is ready, the mark comes alive and hands over to the birds-on-a-wire intro.

  1. Home screenThe Second Strong icon: the heavy S and its clay dot on almond.
  2. Icon opensiOS zooms the icon to fill the screen. The launch screen uses the same almond, so the zoom is seamless.
  3. Launch screenThe big S, centred and still, while the app loads. This is the Instagram-style moment.
  4. The dot comes aliveApp ready: the clay dot drops, bounces, and draws the wire as it lands.
  5. Birds on a wireThe coaches drop onto the wire. Yours comes forward and says morning.
  6. TodayYour coach tucks into the corner and the day is there.

Launch screen options

All still, all built from the icon. iOS uses the dark version when the phone is in dark mode.
