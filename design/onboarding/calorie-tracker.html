Calorie and macro tracker: the experience

What the person would see, in four screens, in the spirit of MacroFactor: a maintenance number that learns from her own weight trend and what she eats, targets for losing fat gently, maintaining or building, and a weekly check-in that adjusts them. Where we differ, on purpose: protein comes first (that is our thesis), every number is an estimate with a range, nothing celebrates weight lost or a deficit kept, and she can hide calories completely. All of it sits inside the safety rules of spec section 7. Everything is interactive and uses fake numbers (Anna, 70 kg, estimated maintenance 1,810). The floor (1,400) and the weekly rates are drafts for a clinician to set. Nothing is built.
