The coach rides the line: proof of concept

Press Continue on any phone (or Replay). The coach rests at the bottom right, hops onto the pen line when it starts drawing, rides it round the answer and down the margin, then settles at the bottom right of the next page as the page scrolls. Three versions of the riding. Nothing is built in the app yet.

Why bottom right: the top right is where the paper badges sit on several pages, so the coach was covering them. The bottom right, just above the Continue button, is the quietest spot, and it is out of the way of the pen's route (which runs down the left).
