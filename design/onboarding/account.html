2 · Buying it for someone you love

The choice sits right after the welcome screen. For me carries on as today. For someone I love takes the short path below: your names, a message, payment, then an invite link to send. She then opens it, signs up on her own phone, answers her own questions, and chooses what (if anything) to share with you. Buying never gives access to her answers (family circle design, D6).

Decided
• Sign-in: Apple, Google and email link. A gifted year carries the 7-day free trial. Price and seats for several people are decided later.

Still to decide
• Whether the stores allow buying a subscription for someone else (Apple has no native subscription gifting), so gifting may need offer codes or a web purchase. Needs a store-rules check.
• When she accepts, does she sign up with the same email you typed, or any email? (I'd say any; the link is the key.)
• Buying for someone is only open to adults; the date-of-birth check for the buyer is not designed yet.
