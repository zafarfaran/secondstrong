Onboarding: working out your numbers

Modelled on how MacroFactor's setup works: after the body questions we ask how active her day is, what she wants, how she wants to see food, and then we work out her maintenance and daily target ourselves and show them. Everything is interactive and shared: change the activity on the first phone, or the goal and pace on the second, and the reveal on the last phone recalculates. Her answers so far are fake (Anna, 54, 165 cm, 70 kg, strength training three days a week). The formula is Mifflin-St Jeor for a woman plus a modest allowance for activity; every factor, the floor and the paces are drafts for a clinician and a nutrition adviser. The switch below shows the safety branch. Nothing is built in the app.
