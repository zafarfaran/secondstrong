Waiting, written in.

Every wait in the app uses the same pen as onboarding. Instead of spinning, the app writes: a stroke underlines, rings, ticks or sketches the thing that's on its way. Each phone loops; replay any of them.

Timing

Launch screens finish in under two seconds and never block: if the app is ready sooner, the sequence skips to its last frame. Longer waits (plan, coach, scan) loop calmly until the result lands, then hand over without a jump.

Motion rules

The pen draws first, words write in after it, ticks and dots pop last. No shimmer, no bouncing dots, no progress percentages. With reduced motion turned on, each screen shows its finished frame.

In the app

On iOS these are Rive files: the pen strokes, the coach states and the timing all live in one state machine, so a loader can jump straight to "done" when data arrives.
