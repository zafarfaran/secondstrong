Food tab, with Miso and some life

The first four were too flat. These four put Miso on the page and move: numbers count up, rings and the jar fill, new meals drop in and Miso reacts. Tap the + on any phone to log the next meal (the fifth one pushes you past your protein goal), Reset under each phone starts again. Miso plays her real clips (thinking, wow, cheer). Calories are optional, so the switch hides them. Food pictures are placeholders; real ones would be scan photos. No glows. Fake data. Nothing is built.
