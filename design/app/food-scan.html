Scanning a meal: four animations

What you see while Miso reads the photo, about five seconds, in three steps: looking, spotting the food, estimating portions. Each loops by itself; Replay restarts it and Slow-mo stretches it so you can study it. All use the same plate photo. Everything is flat paper, no glows. With Reduce Motion on, the app would show D instead of whichever you pick. Fake data. Nothing is built.
