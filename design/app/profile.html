Profile and settings

A small profile button sits top right on Today (and the other tabs). It opens her profile: who she is, her plan, her coach, and every setting in one calm list, grouped and plain. Four screens: A where the button lives, B the profile home, C Your plan and numbers (change height, weight, goal and calories on or off, with the same safe floor), D Privacy and data (export, delete, consent, support). Tap the switches and the profile button; Night shows dark mode. Fake data: Anna, Miso. The Family and billing rows are placeholders for what is decided elsewhere. Nothing is built.
