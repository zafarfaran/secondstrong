Coach: chat, voice, and a coach that messages first

Four screens for the Coach tab, in the spirit of a character you chat and talk with that reaches out to you (the way Muse does): A the chat, where Miso has already written to her; B and C two takes on a voice conversation, tap the screen to cycle listening, thinking and speaking; D how Miso reaches out, with a notification and the controls. All at true iPhone size, in the paper look, fake data. Night shows dark mode. Nothing is built, and nothing here is wired to a real voice or a real coach.
