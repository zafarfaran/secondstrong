Food tab: four layouts

Based on how Cal AI works: a week strip with a ring on each day, one big card for what is left today, a short list of what you ate with photos, and a round + button to scan. Ours differs where it has to: protein comes first, and calories are optional. The Calories switch shows both, because some members (eating-disorder history, pregnant or breastfeeding, or anyone who opted out) never see a calorie number. No shadows. Fake data. Nothing is built. Tap days, the + button and the calendar on each phone.
