Step 4 of 6 · your coach

Pick a coach, then name them.

Construction

Every coach is built the same way: a lit base form, a soft form shadow clipped inside it, a key highlight, then features. One light direction across the cast keeps them reading as a family.

Acting

Brows carry most of the emotion: lifted for hello and listening, one raised for thinking, knitted for lifting, soft and worried for comfort. Eyes, mouth and pose follow.

Handoff

Each layer is a named group (body, shade, eyes, brows, mouth, arms), so the SVGs import into Rive with the rig already separated.
